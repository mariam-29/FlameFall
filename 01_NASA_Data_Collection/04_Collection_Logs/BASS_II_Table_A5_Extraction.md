\## 4. Relationship Between O15a and O15b



\- Both tests use sample 218m.

\- Both use a 9.525-mm clear rod.

\- O15b is explicitly described as a reburn of the same rod used in the previous test.

\- These tests must not be treated as fully independent sample experiments.

\- The extinction settings differ: 0.32 pot in O15a and 0.27 pot in O15b.



\## 5. ProofSheet Cross-Reference



Separate visual reviews were conducted for the O15a and O15b ProofSheets.



\- O15a ProofSheet: Final 16 images show no visible flame.

\- O15b ProofSheet: Final 3 images show no visible flame.

\- In both sequences, the sample remains visible.

\- The absence of visible flame does not independently confirm complete extinction.

\- The ProofSheet dates display July 29, 2014 (GMT 210), while Table A.5 lists July 28, 2014 (GMT 209).

\- Preserve each date as recorded by its respective source.



\## 6. Data Interpretation and Limitations



\- "pot" means potentiometer reading, according to the report footnote.

\- Potentiometer readings must not be interpreted as psi or directly converted into air velocity without a verified conversion.

\- The report mentions 5 cm/s at specific stages, but the pot readings are separate recorded settings.

\- The possible rod heat-up effect in O15b is a question raised by the report, not a confirmed conclusion.

\- Camera metadata is not experimental measurement data.

\- Do not infer exact extinction time from the ProofSheets alone.
