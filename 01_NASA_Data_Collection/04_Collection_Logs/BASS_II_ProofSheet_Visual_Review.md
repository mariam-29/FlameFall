- Investigation: Burning and Suppression of Solids-II (BASS-II)
- Sample: Clear Rod Sample 218M
- Date: July 29, 2014
- Test Points: O15a and O15b
- Source: NASA PSI-25 ProofSheets

## 1. Test Point O15a

- ProofSheet: O15a
- Image sequence: iss040e081891 – iss040e082026
- Date: July 29, 2014

- Sample visibility: The sample remains visible.
- Observation: The sample appearance changes in the final images.
- Interpretation: Possible post-flame or suppression phase.
- Evidence status: Visual observation; phase interpretation is unconfirmed.

## 3. Camera Metadata

- Aperture: f/2.0
- ISO: 1000
- Exposure compensation: -3 EV
- Mode: Aperture Priority
- Shutter speed: Varies between images.

## 4. Important Limitations

- No visible flame does not prove complete extinction.
- Smoke, residual glowing, and cooling are not confirmed.
- Camera metadata must not be treated as experimental measurements.
- No combustion duration or extinction time is inferred from the ProofSheets alone.
